What it is

A finance toolkit that shows its work.

Winners Circle bundles the calculations that come up constantly in financial mathematics — compound growth, annuities, loan amortisation, growing retirement contributions, and interest rate conversion — into one desktop toolkit. Every result comes with a full payment schedule and a chart, not just a single number, so you can check the working, not only the answer.

The Circle

Five modules, one interface.

Each one solves for whichever value you don't have — future value, present value, payment, term, or rate — from the ones you do.

MODULE 01

Single Investment

Grows a lump sum under simple, effective, nominal, or continuous (force of interest) compounding, and lays out a year-by-year balance table alongside it.

MODULE 02

Annuities

Regular payment streams solved for future value, present value, payment size, term, or rate — in arrears or in advance, on the same set of controls.

MODULE 03

Loans

Full amortisation schedules with a running balance, interest and capital split per instalment, and automatic handling of a final payment that doesn't quite match the rest.

MODULE 04

Retirement Growth

Contributions that step up over time — either every k-th period in grouped jumps, or with every single payment — for realistic retirement planning.

MODULE 05

Rate Converter

Moves freely between effective annual, effective periodic, nominal, simple, and continuous rates — the piece everything else quietly depends on.

How it works

Built around the details that usually trip people up.

The kind of edge cases a textbook question tests for, handled by default rather than left for you to catch.

01

Rate compounding vs. payment frequency

A rate that compounds monthly but pays out quarterly is treated as two separate settings, not one — so the conversion is done correctly instead of assumed.

02

Five interest rate types

Effective annual, effective periodic, nominal, simple, and force of interest (δ) — every module accepts whichever one the question gives you.

03

Arrears and advance, side by side

Annuities and loans both offer ordinary and due timing behind the same controls, so switching conventions doesn't mean switching tools.

04

Full schedules, not just totals

Every calculation produces a complete period-by-period table and a matching chart — the working is always one click away from the answer.
